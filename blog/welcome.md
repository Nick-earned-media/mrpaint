Welcome. This is a placeholder post. Replace it with real content — a recently completed project, a seasonal tip, or news from the team.

Blog posts live as markdown files in the `blog/` directory and are automatically picked up at build time. Each post needs three things in its frontmatter:

- `title` — shown on the post page and in the blog index
- `date` — used to sort posts (newest first)
- `summary` — shown on the blog index under the title

Everything below the frontmatter is the post body, written in markdown.
