# The Complete Guide to GEO and AI SEO

## Core Definitions

**GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation)**: Ensuring your content is referenced by AI models like ChatGPT and Gemini when building answers from multiple sources.

**AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation)**: Making your content the direct answer pulled into AI Overviews, appearing in environments like Google's AI results with clear, well-structured responses.

## Key Differences: SEO vs. AEO vs. GEO

| Aspect | SEO | AEO | GEO |
|--------|-----|-----|-----|
| Goal | Top Google ranking | Direct answer provision | AI-generated answer reference |
| Content focus | Keyword-based | Question-based | Query cluster themes |
| Search type | Short keywords | Question searches | Long, conversational prompts |
| Success metric | Rankings & clicks | AI overview inclusion | LLM citations & traffic |
| User path | Click → convert | See answer → may click | See answer → later visit |

## AI Search Process: How Systems Find & Cite Content

1. **Query Expansion (Query Fan-Out)**: AI breaks a single prompt into multiple related queries to understand user intent fully
2. **Web Scanning**: Retrieves relevant content across the web
3. **Content Interpretation**: AI identifies topic, entities, and concept relationships — not just keywords
4. **Source Prioritization**: Narrows results to clear, intent-aligned, credible content with supporting evidence
5. **Dynamic Answer Synthesis**: Combines selected sources into a response (content may be used without direct quotation)
6. **Citation**: References sources depending on platform (Google links sources; ChatGPT varies; Perplexity shows explicit citations)

## Strategic Elements for GEO + AI SEO

### 1. AI Search Intent Research

**Traditional approach**: Start with keywords → research volume/difficulty → create content

**AI approach**: Start with actual search intent → expand with AI tools → group into themes → create comprehensive content

- Review Google Search Console for longer, conversational queries people use
- Use AI tools to generate variations: questions, comparisons, scenario-based prompts
- Group queries into themes aligned with your offerings
- Each theme guides required content creation

### 2. Content Optimization

- **Answer immediately**: Address core question early in the page
- **Use descriptive headings** matching search intent
- **Section structure**: Break articles into focused, short sections
- **Include FAQs** covering related queries
- **Add definitions** for key concepts
- **Use comparison tables** for products/services
- **Provide summaries** or key takeaways throughout

AI systems make early decisions about usefulness — if the answer isn't clear upfront, pages are unlikely to be used.

### 3. Site Structure

Implement a clean hierarchy that AI systems naturally follow:
- Home page (top level)
- 2–3 top-level categories
- Subcategories beneath each
- Sub-subcategories where topic depth warrants

Basic schema markup and proper internal linking ensure crawlability and interpretation.

### 4. Entity Optimization

AI systems connect information across sources. Maintain consistency by:
- Defining positioning, services, and audience once
- Repeating definitions identically across all platforms
- Ensuring consistent business information everywhere

### 5. Third-Party Authority

AI compares multiple sources before inclusion. Strengthen visibility through:
- Link building from trusted industry sites
- Digital PR and media coverage
- Industry roundups and mentions
- Competitor site mentions (when relevant)

These create discovery surfaces and reinforce brand recognition.

## How to Run a GEO & AI Search Audit

### Step 1: Identify Priority Prompts
List realistic buyer prompts using:
- Google Search Console data
- Sales conversation insights
- AI tool-generated variations

Think in scenarios: *"Who's the best physio near me for a running injury?"*

### Step 2: Test Across Platforms
Manually query across: Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot

For each, document:
- Whether your brand appears
- How it's described
- Which competitors are included

### Step 3: Analyze Source Patterns
Examine what AI systems use (own site, competitors, reviews, directories, news, YouTube, forums, reports). High-performing pages are typically structured, answer directly, use clear headings, and align with intent.

### Step 4: Identify Content Gaps
Determine why competitors appear instead of you:
- **Content issues**: Missing pages, too broad/outdated/unclear content
- **Authority issues**: Weak trust signals compared to competitors

### Step 5: Check Technical Issues
- Verify indexing in Google
- Review crawlability, noindex tags, broken links, internal linking
- Ensure key information isn't hidden in tabs, accordions, or images
- Audit site structure and schema implementation

### Step 6: Prioritize by Commercial Value
Start with high-intent prompts (comparisons, pricing, best-of queries) most likely to convert.

## Content Recommendations to Increase AI Citations

### Structure for Extraction
- Lead with direct answer to main question
- Use intent-matching headings
- Create short, focused sections
- Add FAQs
- Include simple concept definitions
- Deploy comparison tables
- Provide summaries/key takeaways

### Build Trust Signals
- Include expert quotes and commentary
- Reference credible studies and statistics
- Add examples or case studies
- Highlight author expertise (years/background)
- Build backlinks from industry sites
- Invest in digital PR and media mentions
- Maintain consistent positioning across platforms

### Create Original Information Gain
- Publish original data from self-run studies
- Include expert perspectives
- Share internal benchmarks
- Create unique frameworks/methodologies
- Comment on emerging trends

**Note**: Human-created content significantly outperforms AI-generated content for AI system citations.

### Use Multimedia
- Comparison tables
- Videos supporting content
- Charts/diagrams for processes
- Screenshots and supporting visuals

AI models increasingly interpret multimodal content.

### Comprehensive Topic Coverage
- Include related questions throughout
- Cover comparisons
- Add scenario-based examples
- Connect pages with internal links
- Update regularly as information evolves

- 26% of brands have zero mentions in AI overviews

## Common Mistakes

- Treating GEO identically to traditional SEO
- Over-focusing on keywords while ignoring structure, authority, and original insights
- Not recognizing that strategy requires human input despite AI tools' utility
